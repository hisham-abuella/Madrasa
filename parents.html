وَعْدُنَا فِي الْأَدَبِ

Every week we practise one Islamic manner in Arabic, inside the lesson: saying salam, sharing, thanking Allah and each other, being gentle. We do not lecture; children act it out in the story and the games.

لَا عَرَبِيَّةَ فِي الْبَيْتِ؟ لَا بَأْسَ

No Arabic at home? You can still help. Every lesson and book page has a listen button, an English line for grown-ups and a short home task you can do together, even if you are learning too.

Accessibility: every child, every family

We want every child to join in. Every Arabic word on this site has a listen button in a real voice; text is large with full vowel marks; buttons are big enough for small fingers; animations stop when your device asks for reduced motion; and nothing is timed, so no child ever runs out of time. Pages work with a keyboard and with VoiceOver, in Arabic and English. If something does not work for your child, please tell us at hello@nawras.example placeholder and we will fix it.
