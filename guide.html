Principles for kids’ art

مَبَادِئُ

Grade 1–2 children read the picture before the word. The picture must be friendly, simple and true to the sentence under it, and it must never carry the Arabic itself.

Shape

Round, chubby, simple shapes. Bold clean dark navy outlines. One subject in the middle with room around it. No clutter, no crowds, no photo-like texture.

Colour

Flat, bright colours, no gradients in stickers. Pictures use the San Diego palette: ocean teal, warm sand, sunset coral, sky blue. UI colour comes from theme tokens (--brand, --accent), never hard-coded.

Faces

Big kind round dark eyes with a white sparkle, rosy cheeks, a small smile. Never scary: scary, angry, sharp teeth are in every negative prompt. One expression per picture.

One character family

Noori, Sami and Salma look the same on every page because their descriptions are pasted, never retyped (section 2). New pictures reuse the book’s STYLE and NEG strings word for word.

No text inside images

The model cannot write: text comes out as nonsense, and Arabic is the worst. Every Arabic word is typeset in HTML in Naskh with full tashkeel. Ask for “a plain blank book”, “a completely plain cover”.

San Diego + Islamic, softened

Ocean, palms, mission arches, seagulls; 8-point stars, zellige, arches, lanterns, crescent and stars. All rounded and bright for kids. Islamic greetings are welcome: السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ.

Content rules: never break these

  • No depictions of prophets or angels. Show what the story is about instead (a place, an object, the children).
  • No Qur’an verses as decoration, inside games, on backgrounds or in generated images.
  • Modest dress on every person: long sleeves, long trousers or a long dress, socks and shoes; girls and women in a headscarf that covers all the hair and neck. Negatives always carry revealing clothing, bare shoulders, bare legs, shorts, swimsuit, bikini, sleeveless, short skirt.
  • No grown-ups in story pictures. The model mangles adult arms and hands. Grown-ups live in the words (the mother on the book’s “fence” page). Only the word pictures teacher-m, teacher-f, mother show adults, modest and kind.
  • Never in a picture: crosses, churches, steeples, temples, idols or statues, pigs, alcohol or bottles, musical instruments. Sound effects are soft tones only; we make no music.
  • No look-alikes of famous characters. The book’s white cat with a pink bow was too close to a famous one, so she became a calico with a pink collar.

The characters

الشَّخْصِيَّاتُ

A local model has no memory of a character. It is held by words alone: one fixed description per character, kept as a shell variable and pasted into every prompt. These are the exact strings from book/source/gen-noori-shell.sh, copied verbatim into assets/art/source/gen-art.sh. Expression and pose go after the variable, never inside it.

A young seagull, from نُور (light). In every story picture, the logo, the stickers and the SVG.

$NOORI

Two or three characters in one picture

Name the positions. Generating images locally

صُنْعُ الصُّوَرِ

All artwork is drawn on the Mac by Z-Image Turbo through draw-things-cli. Nothing goes to a cloud service. About 90 seconds a picture at 1536px, one at a time (the GPU is shared, so never run two batches together; long batches run in the background).

  1. Check the model is there 
    draw-things-cli models list --downloaded-only     # z_image_turbo_1.0_q8p.ckpt must be listed
  2. Add one gen line to the script Book pages go in book/source/gen-<slug>.sh, site pictures in assets/art/source/gen-art.sh. Build the prompt from the variables and end it with the style string. Comment the line if it needed help, so nobody “simplifies” it later.
    Command template
    CLI=/opt/homebrew/bin/draw-things-cli
$CLI generate --model z_image_turbo_1.0_q8p.ckpt \
  --prompt "$NOORI <doing what>, <where>, $STYLE" \
  --negative-prompt "$NEG" \
  --width 1536 --height 1536 --seed <seed> --disable-preview \
  --output assets/art/hi/<group>/<name>.png
    $STYLE (story pictures)
    STYLE="bright cartoon children's book illustration, bold clean dark navy outlines, flat vibrant colours, simple rounded shapes, soft San Diego beach palette of ocean teal, warm sand, sunset coral and sky blue, gentle and cheerful, simple uncluttered background"
    $PSTYLE2 (one object / sticker on white)
    PSTYLE2="bright cartoon children's book illustration, bold clean dark navy outlines, flat vibrant colours, simple rounded shapes, gentle and cheerful, one single object in the centre with empty white space all around it, isolated on a plain solid white background, nothing else in the picture"
    $NEG (every picture)
    NEG="text, letters, words, numbers, arabic script, arabic calligraphy, writing, signs, logo, label, caption, watermark, \
adult, grown-up, man, woman, adult arms, adult hands, extra arms, extra hands, extra fingers, extra limbs, extra wings, extra legs, extra eyes, deformed, \
black bird, crow, duck, chicken, penguin, pelican, long neck, \
idol, statue, cross, church, steeple, temple, pig, alcohol, wine, beer, bottle, revealing clothing, bare shoulders, bare legs, shorts, swimsuit, bikini, sleeveless, short skirt, \
musical instrument, scary, angry, sharp teeth, crowd, cluttered, blurry, photorealistic, photograph, 3d render, painterly, gouache, watercolour texture"

    Add-ons in gen-art.sh: NEG_OBJ (sticker, decal, emoji, badge, teddy bear, bear, bunny, small extra character, tiny person, mascot, speech bubble, face on object, …) for objects; SCN (background scenery, landscape, beach, sea, …) is appended automatically to pic/*, noori/*, levels and word-of-the-day; NEG_BG adds person, people, child, … animal, bird, seagull, face for empty backgrounds; NEG_ADULT removes only the adult words, for the teacher and mother pictures.

  3. Try 3–4 seeds before the batch 
    # book page: draws build/<slug>-try/<page>-<seed>.png + a sheet to pick from
cd book && zsh tools/try-seeds.sh noori-shell <page> <first-seed> 4
# site picture: redraw with the next seed block, into assets/art/hi/tries/
SEED_ADD=100 ONLY="pic/cat" zsh assets/art/source/gen-art.sh
    If the same drift shows on every seed, change the words, not the seed.
  4. Review on a contact sheet Then open anything suspicious at full size (count limbs on the full picture, never on a 300px tile). 
    python3 book/tools/contact-sheet.py build/sheet.png assets/art/hi/pic/*.png   # --cols 4 --tile 300

    The book’s sheet (book/build/noori-shell-sheet.png). Side by side, a changed face or a missing scarf jumps out.

  5. Eye-check every picture The list the book and site pictures were checked against:
    • Same face, hair colour and cut, same outfit; Noori’s teal scarf is there.
    • Modest dress: no bare shins, shoulders or arms; headscarf covers all the hair and neck.
    • No text, letters, numbers or logo-like squiggles anywhere (book covers, signs, shirts).
    • No extra characters: a bear, a bunny, a sticker boy, a speech bubble with a face.
    • Limbs and wings counted; no merged characters (seed 609 merged the she-cat into Noori).
    • The picture matches the words (seed 606: Noori hugging the trunk, the text says he sits on top).
    • No grown-ups (except the three word pictures), nothing from the content rules.
    • Style matches: flat cartoon, not photo-like (story s05-dusk seed 1205 was rejected for a realistic pier).
    • Empty space is where the page needs it (sand for stickers, sky for a headline).
  6. Log it Put the winning seed on the gen line, a comment above it saying what the rejected seed did wrong, and a line in the script’s REVIEW LOG. Rejected tries go to hi/tries/<group>-<name>-<seed>.png (site) or build/<slug>-try/ (book).
  7. Make the web copies Pages never reference a master. 
    python3 assets/art/source/make-web.py          # site: WebP, stickers cut out to transparent
cd book && bash tools/web-art.sh noori-shell    # book: 1024px copies of the 1536px masters

Folders and names

  • Masters: assets/art/hi/<group>/<name>.png (89 MB, never linked).
  • Web: assets/art/<group>/<name>.webp; groups pic noori story site.
  • The file name is the API name: KS.pic('cat-f') loads pic/cat-f.webp; KS.gull('wave') loads noori/wave.webp.
  • Lowercase, hyphens, English keys; story slides s04-wall.
  • Book: book/art-hi/images-<slug>/<page>.png → book/images-<slug>/.

Size budget

  • Every site WebP ≤ 150 KB (make-web.py steps quality 86 → 44 until it fits). Real files: stickers 15–30 KB, story 25–65 KB, heroes 42–75 KB.
  • The book’s web copies are 1024px PNGs, about 1 MB each (17 MB for 16): fine for the book, too heavy for a lesson page. Use WebP for anything new.
  • loading="lazy", decoding="async", width and height on every <img>.

Regeneration log: what went wrong and the words that fixed it

The horseshoe-arch lesson (logos)

“An emblem shaped like an Andalusian horseshoe arch” drew a literal horseshoe on all four seeds (201–204). The model takes the common meaning of a word. The fix was to describe the shape, not name it: “a Moorish arched window like the Alhambra: a tall pointed keyhole-shaped arch with a rounded top, standing on two slim columns and a flat base”, plus horseshoe, horse, magnet, lucky charm, U shape in NEG. Seed 253 won. The script says: do not “simplify” this back to “horseshoe arch”.

Also redrawn: nakhla 606 → 706 (spelled “sitting among the green leaves at the very top”), sadiq 610 → 710 (Sami got a headscarf), noori/fly 1102 (stood on the ground), noori/think 1103 (no wing on the chin), noori/sleep 1105 (eyes open), pic/sea 1020 (a bear in the sea), pic/door 1010 (a bear by the door), level-kg 1310 (a speech bubble with a face). The full reasons are comments in the two scripts.

Drawing in code (inline SVG)

الرَّسْمُ بِالْبَرْمَجَةِ

The site started 100% inline SVG: no broken images, no emoji. The generated set sits on top of it with an artwork switch, and the SVG is always the fallback.

Use SVG for

  • The logo in the top bar (.logo always gets the SVG gull), icons, ornaments, patterns.
  • Anything that animates by its parts: Noori’s eyes blink, wings flap, head tilts.
  • Small sizes (under ~64px) and the theme-coloured UI.
  • Print pages: pin them with <html data-art-fixed data-art="svg">.
  • The fallback when a picture is missing.

Use generated art for

  • Word pictures in cards and quizzes (KS.pic).
  • Story scenes, book pages, heroes, level cards, word of the day.
  • The Noori sticker set (KS.gull in illustrated mode).
  • Anywhere a child should feel “this is a real picture book”.

Colour with CSS variables so themes swap

SVG parts carry a class, the class reads a token, every theme sets the token. Press a theme and watch the same drawing change.

kids.css
.gull .g-scarf { fill: var(--gull-scarf); }   /* pacific #11A5A9 · andalus #3B7A4C · mosaic #2450D8 */
.gull .g-line  { stroke: var(--gull-line); stroke-width: 2.6; }
.pic .f-fem    { fill: var(--fem); }          /* clothes follow the theme's feminine colour */
.pic .f-hijab  { fill: color-mix(in srgb, var(--fem) 40%, #FFFFFF); }
KS.gull('happy', {svg:true})

The artwork switch and the fallback

رُسُومٌ بَسِيطَةٌ (simple, SVG) ↔ رُسُومٌ مُلَوَّنَةٌ (illustrated, the default). It lives in the theme picker; KS.art() reads it, KS.art('svg'|'ill') sets it and saves it in localStorage['nawras-art']. In illustrated mode KS.pic / KS.gull return an <img> whose onerror calls KS.artFallback(this) and swaps in the SVG, so a missing file never shows a broken image.

data-gull="wave"

This <img> was given a broken file. onerror turned it into the SVG shell. The same happens when a lesson uses a word whose picture is not drawn yet (a missing file).

Typography with images

الْخَطُّ وَالصُّورَةُ

Every children’s word is HTML text in Noto Naskh Arabic (--f-word, class .word) with full tashkeel, under or beside the picture, never inside it. Line height 1.9 leaves room for the harakat. Never letter-space Arabic.

هَذِهِ بِنْتٌ

This is a girl.

هَذَا وَلَدٌ

This is a boy.

Rules

  • Picture above, word below, English caption smallest and last.
  • هَذَا uses --masc-text, هَذِهِ uses --fem-text, the ة uses --ta.
  • Wrap English inside Arabic in <bdi> or .en (it isolates direction).

Colouring the ة without breaking the word: the ZWJ trick

Putting part of an Arabic word in its own <span> can cut the join between the letters (the letter before the span takes its end form). A zero-width joiner (&zwj;, U+200D) on both sides of the split keeps them joined. KS.ta(word) does this for the trailing ة; [data-ta] elements are wired automatically. Test on an iPad: browsers differ in how they shape across a span.

مُعَلِّمَةٌ

Span, no ZWJ: the join can break.

مُعَلِّمَ<span class="ta">ةٌ</span>

KS.ta(): ZWJ on both sides, joined.

مُعَلِّمَ&zwj;<span class="ta">&zwj;ةٌ</span>

Animation rules

الْحَرَكَةُ

Motion has a job: feedback (you tapped, you answered), attention (look here), or reward (you did it). If it has no job, it goes. Every demo below runs the real class or function from the kit.

Applies the same rules kids.css uses for prefers-reduced-motion: reduce to the demos below.

Reduced motion is on

Every animation and transition jumps to its end state (0.001 ms, one iteration), loops stop, confetti is skipped, scroll reveals and the tagline appear at once. The child still gets the star, the colour, the sound and the words: the reward never depends on motion.

Pop-in

.anim-pop · popIn · 420ms (--t-pop) · --ease-pop

Job: something new arrives: a star, a card, the result.

Wiggle

.anim-wiggle · wiggle ±6deg · .5s · ease-in-out · once

Job: attention: “tap me”. Once, not on a loop.

Hop (joy)

KS.cheer(el) · hop .5s ×2 --ease-pop + flapL/flapR .3s ×4 alternate

Job: reward after a right answer or انْتَهَيْتُ. Tapping Noori anywhere does it too.

Wrong = gentle shake

بِنْتٌ

.is-bad · shake 10px→4px · .4s · ease · once + KS.sfx('bad') (soft triangle tones)

Job: feedback. Then a hint, never “wrong!”, no red X, no lost stars; try again.

The ة flies in

.ta-fly · taFly .9s --ease-pop, then .ta-glow · glow 1.4s ×2

Job: teaches مُعَلِّمٌ + ة = مُعَلِّمَةٌ. The ة lands in the word.

Confetti

For finishing something: the quiz (40, or 80 for all right) and the writing pad (40).

KS.confetti(n=70) · fall 1.3–2.5s ease-out, delay ≤ .35s · removed after 3.2s · theme --c1…--c4

Job: big reward, rarely. Never on every answer.

Noori: blink, flap, moods

blink 5s ∞ (eyes shut at 96%) · fly: flapL/R .5s ∞ alternate · wave 1.1s ∞ · think: tilt 2.4s ∞ · cheer: hop .6s ×3

Job: Noori feels alive. Loops are slow and small; they stop under reduced motion.

Word-by-word tagline

نَتَعَلَّمُ الْفُصْحَى بِفَرَحٍ

[data-words] · wordIn .6s --ease-pop · stagger 220ms + 200ms

Job: the hero line arrives the way it is read. Split on spaces only, never inside a word; aria-label keeps the whole sentence.

Easing: why things “land”

--ease-pop cubic-bezier(.34, 1.56, .64, 1)
--ease-out cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
linear (don’t)

Use: --ease-pop overshoots a little, for things that arrive or reward; --ease-out for moves and fades. All over --t-move 600ms here.

Rules

  • Motion has a job (feedback, attention, reward). Feedback finishes in under a second.
  • Nothing flashes more than 3 times a second. The fastest repeat in the kit is a wing flap (.3s alternate) which moves, not flashes; glow pulses every 1.4s.
  • Honour prefers-reduced-motion. kids.css already does it globally; new keyframes need nothing extra. JS effects check it too (KS.confetti returns early).
  • No timers, countdowns or punishment. No “time’s up”, no losing stars, no lives. A wrong answer gets a gentle shake, a soft tone and a hint, then the right answer is shown.
  • One thing moves at a time where the child is looking. Ambient loops (blink, float) stay small and slow.
  • Animate transform and opacity only. Re-run a class with el.classList.remove(c); void el.offsetWidth; el.classList.add(c).

Settings and tags

transcript JSON (one per language, the source of truth)
{ "title": "…", "language": "ar", "voice": "EXAVITQu4vr4xnSDxMaL", "model": "eleven_v4",
  "settings": { "stability": 0.5, "similarity_boost": 0.8 },
  "slides": [
    { "id": "s2", "title": "هذا وهذه",
      "transcript": "[playfully] اُنظُروا مَعي… <break time=\"0.4s\" /> هذا وَلَدٌ، وهذهِ بِنتٌ. <break time=\"0.5s\" /> هذا قِطٌّ، وهذهِ قِطَّةٌ." } ] }
  • eleven_v4, stability 0.5, similarity 0.8: the house recipe. v3/v4 ignore the style dial, so delivery goes into tags.
  • Tags in English, in brackets, one per sentence: [warmly] [softly] [gently] [playfully] [curious] [excited] [whispers]. Breaks <break time="0.4s" /> give children time to look at the picture.
  • Add tags programmatically around the existing text, never retype a corrected line.
  • Read in sequence: generate.js sends the neighbouring slides as previous_text / next_text.

Arabic rules for the voice

  • Screen and voice differ. On screen: full tashkeel always. In a TTS script: selective tashkeel on ambiguous words (قِطَّة, يُصبِح); full tashkeel everywhere can confuse the model.
  • Carrier sentences. A bare sound or a lone letter warbles; put it in a sentence: البقرةُ تقول: مُوو! A lone ة was read «تاء» in the book (caught by Whisper), so say التّاءُ المَربوطة.
  • Never invent onomatopoeia; for real noises use the sound-effects endpoint, not a voice.
  • Spell out numbers; no symbols, URLs or digits in a script.
  • Arabic runs 30–50% longer than English; an Arabic clip shorter than its English twin is in the wrong folder.
  • Fish S2-Pro softens emphatic letters in Arabic (ق→ك, ص→س, ط→ت, ض→ظ): English only for anything that ships; listen to every Egyptian clip if the local model is tried.

Writing pad & print exercise art

لَوْحُ الْكِتَابَةِ وَالطِّبَاعَةُ

Every lesson has a writing station: trace the ة, trace هَذَا / هَذِهِ, write a short sentence under a picture. On screen it is KS.writePad; in print it is dotted words on ruled lines.

<div data-writepad data-model="ة|هَذِهِ" data-lines="2"></div>
KS.writePad(el, { model: ['ة', 'هَذَا'], lines: 2, lineHeight: 140 })   // → { clear, undo, toPNG, redraw }

On screen

  • Finger, mouse or Apple Pencil (pressure sets the width); touch-action: none; touches ignored for 1.5s after the pen (palm).
  • Baseline at 72% of the line, dotted mid-line at 42%; model word in Naskh at half the line height, dotted, from the right, with a green start dot.
  • Three pens from tokens (--ink, --masc-text, --fem-text), eraser, undo, clear, show/hide model, save PNG, 52px buttons.
  • انْتَهَيْتُ always praises: three stars, sfx('good'), 40 confetti, Noori hops. No grading of handwriting, ever.

In print

  • Ruled lines ≥ 12 mm per line for small hands: .write-lines uses 14 mm; the book uses 26 mm with an 8 mm letter body.
  • Dotted tracing words (grey-blue #7F8DB0, dash 2.5/3) with a green start dot (#2F9E44) and arrows, measured from the real glyphs.
  • White pages, thin lines, no tints behind writing: they must take pencil and crayon. Don’t laminate.
  • Pictures on exercise pages are the SVG word pictures or simple outlines; a “draw it” box gets a caption line.
  • One QR (≥ 25 mm, quiet zone, dark on white) per exercise page, linking to its writing-pad page.
