Nawras Fusha School, San Diego — “Fusha for little hearts, by the Pacific.” This is the demo hub for the school owners: six homepage ideas, the Noori mascot, logo concepts, one sample lesson told four ways (article, story, slides and a printed lesson book), and the presentation. Every page has a theme button (bottom corner) so any look can be tried on any layout.

سِتَّةُ نَمَاذِجَ لِلصَّفْحَةِ الرَّئِيسَةِ

Six homepage mockups — each a different layout and look
01

الْأَنْدَلُسُ Andalus garden

Horseshoe arch over a fountain garden, zellige bands, terracotta + garden green. San Diego’s mission arches, traced back to Al-Andalus.
flagship
02

الْمُحِيطُ Pacific coast

Full-bleed sunset ocean, palms and Noori flying in. The most playful — and the most “San Diego”.
03

الْفَانُوسُ Night lanterns

Lapis night, a crescent and gold lanterns that light up when tapped. Calm and a little magical.
04

الْكُرَّاسَةُ Madrasa notebook

The page is a notebook: index tabs, sticky notes, a flashcard, crayon doodles — and a writing pad to trace on.
05

الْفُسَيْفِسَاءُ Mosaic playground

A bento grid of chunky tiles, 8-point stars, bold primaries, thick outlines and offset shadows.
06

الْمِحْرَابُ Soft mihrab

White, pastel and quiet: one rounded arch and soft cards. Speaks to parents and older kids.
