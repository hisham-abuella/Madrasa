The idea

Proper Arabic has only our hours, so one lesson, written once, reaches the child four ways

  • on a screen at home
  • on the classroom TV with the teacher
  • in a printed book they write in
  • read aloud in their ears

Teachers teach. Nawras makes it joyful.

Our students live in English, and at home in a dialect. Fusha, the language of the Qur'an, gets only the hours they spend with us. So those hours must be joyful. Let me ask you, as parents: when your own child comes to Arabic class, do they run in, or drag their feet? Here is our idea. We write a lesson once, carefully, in proper Arabic. The child gets it four ways: on a screen at home, on the classroom TV with the teacher, in a printed book they write in, and read aloud in their ears. Teachers teach; Nawras makes it joyful.

— مصري —

ولادنا عايشين بالإنجليزي، وفي البيت بلهجة. والفصحى، لغة القرآن، مالهاش غير الساعات اللي معانا. يعني الساعات دي لازم تبقى فرحة. خلّوني أسألكم كأهالي: لما ابنكم أو بنتكم بييجوا حصة العربي، بيجروا على الفصل ولا بيتسحبوا؟ فكرتنا بسيطة. بنكتب الدرس مرة واحدة، بعناية، وبعربي سليم. والطفل ياخده بأربع طرق: على الشاشة في البيت، وعلى شاشة الفصل مع المدرّس، وفي كتاب مطبوع يكتب فيه، ومسموع في ودانه. المدرّسين بيعلّموا، ونَوْرَس بيخلّي التعليم فرحة.

Children write with a finger or an Apple Pencil, and are praised, never marked wrong

  • Trace the ة, then هَذَا / هَذِهِ, then a sentence under a picture
  • Start at the green dot, right to left
  • Big buttons: pen colours, eraser, undo, clear, save
  • انْتَهَيْتُ "I finished" gives a star; no auto-grading

Try it

Tapping is not enough; children must write. On an iPad the child traces the shell letter, then hadhihi, with a finger or an Apple Pencil, from the green dot, right to left. The buttons are big: colours, eraser, undo, clear. When they press "I finished", they get a star. We never grade handwriting automatically and never mark it wrong; that judgement stays with the teacher.

  • Modest clothing on every child
  • No music, only soft sounds

What Nawras is not

  • Not a replacement for the teacher
  • Not an app, no subscription for families
  • Not auto-grading of handwriting
  • Not yet a full curriculum: one lesson so far

You care about adab, and so do we. These rules are written into everything we make. No pictures of prophets or angels. No verses of the Qur'an as decoration or inside a game. Modest clothing on every child; when a picture came out wrong, we drew it again. No music, only soft sounds. And a word about what Nawras is not. It does not replace your teachers: the teacher presents and judges the writing. It is not an app, and families pay no subscription. And today it is one lesson that shows the format, not a full curriculum.
