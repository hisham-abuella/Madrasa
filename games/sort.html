لِلْأَهْلِ وَالْمُعَلِّمِينَ For parents and teachers

Ten pictures, one at a time. Noori says each word; your child taps the هَذَا basket (masculine) or the هَذِهِ basket (feminine). Three “tricky” feminine words with no ة — بِنْتٌ girl, أُمٌّ mother, شَمْسٌ sun — are always mixed in, so the rule “ة usually means feminine” is learned together with its exceptions. Tries are unlimited and there is no score or timer: the first wrong tap gives a clue (look at the end of the word), the second one models the sentence and lights up the right basket. It suits children who cannot trace or read yet. Tap the speaker on the card to hear the word again. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
