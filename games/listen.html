لِلْأَهْلِ وَالْمُعَلِّمِينَ For parents and teachers

Eight words. Noori says a word (the picture stays hidden); your child listens to the end of the word and taps هَذَا (masculine) or هَذِهِ (feminine). Then the picture appears and Noori says the whole sentence. Two tricky feminine words without ة (بِنْتٌ، أُمٌّ، شَمْسٌ) are mixed in. Tap “Listen” as often as you like. No timer and nothing is lost: the first wrong tap replays the word, the second shows the picture and the written word, the third models the sentence and lights up the answer; every star is earned. If your device has no Arabic voice, the word appears in a bubble instead (recorded word clips are coming). Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
