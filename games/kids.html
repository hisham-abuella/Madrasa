لِلْأَهْلِ وَالْمُعَلِّمِينَ For parents and teachers

This screen is for children aged about 4–6 who cannot read yet. Every choice is a big picture; the first tap makes Noori say it (a recorded clip or the device’s Arabic voice, or a word bubble if there is none), the second tap opens it. The story opens in its short “point and say” version for little ones (6 pages, no grammar words); older children can use the full story from the hub. The three picture games need no reading: pictures, sound and two big buttons.

Accessibility. Every Arabic word has a listen button, buttons are at least 56 px for small fingers, text grows with your browser’s zoom, animations stop when your device asks for reduced motion, and nothing depends on colour alone. Noori never uses a timer and never marks a child wrong. If something does not work for your child — a screen reader, a switch, a missing voice — please tell us at hello@nawras.example (placeholder address) and we will fix it.
