لِلْأَهْلِ وَالْمُعَلِّمِينَ For parents and teachers

Eight pictures. Your child builds the sentence under the picture one tile at a time, right to left: first هَذَا or هَذِهِ, then the word for the picture (beside a look-alike such as قِطٌّ / قِطَّةٌ), and in the last four pictures the describing word صَغِيرٌ or صَغِيرَةٌ (small), which agrees with the noun: هَذِهِ بِنْتٌ صَغِيرَةٌ. Noori reads the finished sentence back. A wrong tile shakes gently and Noori gives a hint; after two tries the right tile glows. No timer, every star is earned. It is the “build” step between choosing and writing. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
