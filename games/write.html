لِلْأَهْلِ وَالْمُعَلِّمِينَ For parents and teachers

Three levels: single letters (ا ب ت ث ن ه ة), joined forms (ـة, ـهـ, بـة) and words from the lesson (هَذَا، هَذِهِ، بِنْتٌ، قِطَّةٌ) — the body first, right to left, then the dots, then the harakat. In Watch, Noori draws each stroke slowly with a numbered green start dot and an arrow; use Replay, Next step and the speed buttons. In Write, your child draws each stroke in the same order on the faint guide. A stroke is checked gently (start near the dot, same direction, close to the line): a good stroke turns green; otherwise it fades, Noori shows that step again and gives a hint, and after three tries Noori helps. No timer, nothing is “wrong”, and stars come at the end. Works with a finger, Apple Pencil (pressure) or a mouse. Sound is off until you turn it on (the speaker button). Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
